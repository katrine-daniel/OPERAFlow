Travel operations automation

One operations desk for hotel bookings, suppliers, reports and booking-site QA.

OPERAFlow brings online and offline bookings into a single registry, chases suppliers who haven't replied, sends the daily operations report on schedule, and tests the booking website end to end — with one secure backend, one audit trail and role-based access for the whole team.

What it automates

Four jobs that usually live in spreadsheets, inboxes and manual checks — sharing one database, one login and one reporting engine.

01

Booking registry

Website bookings (signed webhook) and offline bookings from TBO, hotel calls and supplier email in one list, with duplicate detection and reconciliation.

02

Supplier follow-ups

Tracks quotation and rate-request emails in Gmail or Microsoft 365 and drafts a follow-up when a supplier hasn't replied in 24/48/72 hours.

03

Daily reports

Excel and PDF operations reports on a schedule in your timezone, delivered only to approved recipients, with delivery history.

04

Booking-site testing

Playwright journeys — search, rates, guest details, book, cancel — on desktop and mobile, with screenshots, traces and alerts.

Every booking in one registry

Online and offline bookings side by side, searchable and filterable by channel, source, supplier, status and dates.

  • Internal references, supplier references and confirmation numbers
  • Margin is calculated — and left blank, never zero, when price or cost is missing
  • Website-owned fields are locked; staff edit only what they own
  • Field-level change history with who, when and what

Offline bookings, done properly

A guided form for TBO, direct-hotel and supplier-email bookings. If the same guest, hotel and dates already exist — even with the name typed differently — staff are warned before saving.

  • Missing information is flagged on the booking and in reports
  • Vouchers and confirmations attached securely
  • CSV/Excel bulk import with a preview before anything is written

Reconciliation catches what people miss

Compares online and offline records: possible duplicates, the same stay booked twice at different prices, a cancellation that reached the website but not the offline record, and bookings missing data.

  • Issues resolve themselves when the data is fixed
  • Dismissed issues stay dismissed, with the reason recorded
  • Runs nightly, or on demand after an import

Supplier follow-ups that wait for a human

When a supplier hasn't replied, OPERAFlow drafts the follow-up and asks the owner to approve the exact text. Out-of-office replies don't count as answers; bounces and unknown senders go to review.

  • Business hours, weekends and timezones respected
  • Only the supplier's approved contacts are ever emailed
  • Unattended sending only after an administrator approves the rule
  • If the supplier replies before approval, nothing is sent

The daily report, on time, every day

Bookings made (or arriving) each day in your timezone, with totals per currency, data-quality warnings and open reconciliation issues — as Excel and PDF.

  • Preview before a schedule can be switched on
  • Approved recipients only; a summary can also go to Teams or Slack
  • Each delivery tracked; retries never email anyone twice

The booking website, tested end to end

Real browser journeys on desktop and mobile. Each run records every step, timing, screenshots and a replayable trace; failures are explained in plain language and critical ones alert the team.

  • Bookings are created or cancelled only in a sandbox that allows it
  • Real card numbers are refused; only published test cards are typed
  • The browser can't leave the site under test

Honest about every connection

Each channel and integration shows exactly how it is connected. Nothing is shown as live until real data has arrived; demo and manual channels are labelled as such.

  • Signed webhook for the booking website
  • Gmail and Microsoft 365 via OAuth; Teams/Slack channels
  • Every sign-in and change in an append-only audit log

Works on the phone too

The whole application adapts to small screens, so managers can check confirmations, approve follow-ups and read the daily report on the move.

Built for real guest data

Designed for internal office use with personal information, payments and supplier relationships in mind.

Role-based access

Administrator, Operations Manager, Operations Staff and QA — enforced on the server for every request. QA never sees guest data.

Secure sessions

Argon2id passwords, lockout after repeated failures, revocable server-side sessions, rate limiting and CSRF protection.

Encrypted secrets

Mailbox tokens, webhook secrets and test credentials are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and never shown again.

Nothing invented

Missing amounts stay missing and are reported. Totals are per currency, never mixed or converted.

No duplicate sends

Reports and follow-ups are idempotent: a retry can never email the same person twice.

Audited and monitored

Append-only audit log, request IDs on every response, health checks, metrics and data-retention rules.
